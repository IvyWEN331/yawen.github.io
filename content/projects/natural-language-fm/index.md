This project develops an **LLM-assisted, Digital Twin-linked Knowledge Graph for facility management**, transforming fragmented Operations & Maintenance manuals into structured, queryable, and traceable operational knowledge.

The framework integrates **IFC-based asset information, maintenance procedures, document provenance, and BMS energy data** within a unified Knowledge Graph, enabling facility managers to interact with building information through natural-language queries.

A real-building case study demonstrates how the system can retrieve **asset-specific maintenance procedures**, trace answers back to the original manual pages, and connect operational energy readings to individual Digital Twin assets. The approach reduces manual document navigation and supports more transparent, evidence-grounded, and auditable FM decision-making.

### Key highlights

- LLM-assisted extraction of maintenance knowledge from O&M manuals.
- Asset-level alignment with IFC-based Digital Twin objects.
- Explicit document provenance for traceable and auditable answers.
- Integration of BMS energy and performance data.
- Natural-language interaction for maintenance and operational queries.
- Demonstrated through a real-building case study.
