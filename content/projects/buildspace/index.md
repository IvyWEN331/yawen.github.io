BUILDSPACE is a Horizon Europe research project investigating digital building representations across multiple pilot sites in Europe. My work at the University of Cambridge focuses on **Digital Twin generation and updating**, combining UAV and 360° imagery, point clouds, BIM/IFC information, computer vision, and semantic enrichment.

The research addresses a practical question: how can heterogeneous observations of real buildings be transformed into structured and updateable digital information that supports downstream building applications?
