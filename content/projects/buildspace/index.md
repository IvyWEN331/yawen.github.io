BUILDSPACE is a Horizon Europe project focused on the development of **Digital Twin construction and updating workflows for the built environment**. My work develops a scan-to-BIM pipeline that connects reality capture with automated geometric and semantic modelling.

The workflow integrates **laser scanning, photogrammetry, point-cloud processing, Machine Learning, Large/Visual Language Models, and automated IFC generation** to transform captured building information into structured Digital Twin models.

The workflow has been implemented across **five buildings in four European countries — Poland, Latvia, Greece, and Slovenia** — demonstrating its adaptability across different building types, data conditions, and operational contexts.

### Key highlights

- End-to-end workflow from physical capture to Digital Twin construction.
- Integration of point clouds, imagery, ML/CV, L/VLMs, and IFC.
- Automated geometric and semantic information extraction.
- Validation across five buildings in four European countries.
- Demonstrates scalable Digital Twin modelling from building to broader urban contexts.
