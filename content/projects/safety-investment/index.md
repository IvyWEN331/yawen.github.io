My doctoral research developed an integrated framework for optimising safety investment allocation in construction site management. The work linked **accident-cause knowledge extraction, probabilistic risk assessment, quality function deployment, and multi-objective optimisation** to support evidence-based allocation of limited safety resources.

The research reflects a broader interest in translating heterogeneous built-environment information into transparent and actionable decision support.
